Vought privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026.

Vought is a personal app. Its only user is its owner, and the only Google account it connects to is the owner's own.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Vought reads, labels, archives and drafts email in the owner's Gmail account. It sends email only when the owner approves that message.

Where the data goes

  • Email content is sent to the Anthropic Claude API to sort mail and write drafts.
  • Short summaries are sent to the owner through Telegram.
  • Message headers, short snippets, summaries and drafts are stored in a database on the owner's own computer, and in the owner's private backups.

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can remove Vought's access at any time in Google Account settings, under Security.

Contact

Questions go to the owner through GitHub.
